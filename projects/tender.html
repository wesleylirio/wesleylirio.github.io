AI-Assisted Tender Analysis (POC)

Automation pipeline for procurement requirement analysis, cross-reference matching, and structured spreadsheet output.

Summary

Proof-of-concept aimed at reducing manual analysis time in public/private tender evaluation workflows through AI-assisted extraction.

Context

Procurement teams spend significant effort reading dense documents, identifying requirements, and mapping them to internal catalog items.

Problem

The process is repetitive, error-prone, and difficult to scale when large numbers of documents must be reviewed quickly.

Solution

Designed a pipeline that extracts requirements, compares with existing product catalogs, and outputs structured data ready for review.

Technical Approach

Combined document parsing, LLM-based information extraction, rule-based validation, and export routines for .xlsx generation.

Outcome

The POC demonstrated viable acceleration of analysis cycles and improved consistency in early-stage procurement decision support.

Tech Stack

Node.js LLM APIs Automation Spreadsheet Export REST APIs
